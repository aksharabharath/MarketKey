Self-Inspection Checklist

Initial each item after confirming it is ready for operation.

1. Hand-wash station set up first: 100°F water, free-flow spigot, catch bucket, soap, and paper towels.
2. Utensil wash station set up with wash, rinse, and sanitizer buckets.
3. Tasks delegated to prevent cross-contamination and maintain a clean prep area.
4. All food preparation is done inside the booth.
5. Food cooked outdoors is served to customers from inside the booth.
6. A thermometer is available to measure food temperatures.
7. Cold foods are well iced and below 45°F.
8. Hot foods are served directly, held at or above 135°F, or discarded at day’s end.
9. The health permit is prominently displayed in public view.
10. Trash containers are available inside the booth.
11. Self-service condiments are protected with suitable containers or packets.
12. Open food is protected from coughs and sneezes, and workers are healthy.
13. All four booth walls are in place unless all food is pre-packaged.
14. The locations of clean water, grey water, and fry oil disposal sites are known.
