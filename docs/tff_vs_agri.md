Path 1: Temporary Food Facility
- vendors selling baked goods, hot foods, anyone handing out food samples
- uses the county form
- must go through the full 37 questions

Path 2: agricultural route
- local farmers (fruits, veggies, honey)
- does not use the county form
- instead of opening a permit form, the app routes them to an agriculture exemption guidance view - they are exempt from standard TFF permits
