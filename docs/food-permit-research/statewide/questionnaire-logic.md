# Proposed Statewide Questionnaire Logic

This is a product design proposal, not an official legal decision tree.

1. **Location:** Collect full address, city, ZIP, and county; resolve the actual LEA. Do not assume every city is served by the county agency.
2. **Preparation/storage:** Private residence; commercial facility; shared kitchen/commissary; truck/trailer; cart/stand; temporary booth; other.
3. **Food:** Exact products, ingredients, potentially hazardous status, packaging, storage, preparation process, and specialized processes.
4. **Sales:** Direct/indirect, delivery, event sales, annual gross sales, meals/day/week where relevant. Use adjusted caps only after validating current CDPH notices.
5. **Facility status:** New construction, remodel, equipment/layout/menu change, or change of ownership. Trigger plan-review guidance using HSC § 114380 and current LEA requirements.
6. **Customer handoff:** Premises, delivery, catering/private event, host facility, public event, truck, cart/stand.

## Conditional routing
- Home + eligible shelf-stable product → check CFO list and class/sales channel.
- Home + prepared meals → check local MEHKO authorization and current restrictions.
- Commercial premises → local retail food facility permit and plan review screening.
- Truck/trailer → MFF screening.
- Cart/stand → CMFO screening.
- Community event booth → TFF screening.
- Packaged/processed products outside CFO scope → evaluate local permit versus CDPH PFR.

## Result page
Show likely pathway(s), why they may fit, unanswered questions, responsible LEA, official links, confirmed documents, and fees/timelines only when supported by current official schedules. State clearly that the result is informational, not a permit or approval.

Do not hard-block users based on unverified caps, MEHKO availability, CFO eligibility, MFF/CMFO exemptions, TFF day counts, credential exceptions, or local fees/timelines.
