# Food Safety Credentials and Operating Standards

## Food handler training
CDPH's Retail Food Safety Training document says HSC § 113948 requires food handlers to obtain and maintain a valid food handler card during employment in a retail food establishment; it says the card is valid for three years. Applicability and exceptions depend on statutory definitions and facility type. Do not show this as a universal requirement for every home-based or packaged-food business.

## Person in Charge / food protection manager
CDPH says HSC § 113947 requires a person functioning as Person in Charge of a retail food establishment to have more in-depth training. Check the current code and facility-specific exceptions before deciding who must hold a certificate. The supplied Gemini report says five-year validity; confirm the exact current rule before coding the duration.

## CFO training is distinct
CDPH's CFO training page says anyone preparing or packaging cottage food must complete a food processor course within three months of registration/permit and every three years. CDPH says ANSI-accredited food handler courses can satisfy this requirement.

## Temperature controls
The supplied Gemini report cites cold holding at or below 41°F and hot holding at or above 135°F. This baseline has not yet checked every relevant code section, exception, time-as-a-public-health-control rule, cooling/reheating rule, or specialized process. Do not build a compliance checker from these two values alone.

## Specialized processes
CDPH's HACCP-at-retail guidance identifies specialized activities such as smoking food as a preservation method and curing. Such activities can require a HACCP plan and LEA review. Ask about specialized processing when relevant, then direct the user to the LEA.
